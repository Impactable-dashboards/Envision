What the ICP filter surfaces

59 ICP companies and 30 named ICP buyers are already on the website, and three have had paid exposure.

Method. The full industry distribution was pulled across all 1,306 identified companies, then every ICP industry bucket exhaustively, then the mislabelled buckets swept, then people filtered by seniority and by department rather than by session count. That produces a complete ICP set rather than a volume-ranked sample, which matters because every ICP company here has only one to three visits and a volume ranking buries them beneath high-frequency job seekers.

Result. 59 ICP companies, 30 named ICP people across 29 accounts, and 3 companies with confirmed paid exposure. Because the ad-exposure list only reaches companies above roughly 235 impressions, 3 matched is a floor and the unmatched set is a ceiling.

The account to watch. Moroccanoil appears in both the July ad cohort and on the website, with two named marketing managers, so at least one account already carries all three signals end to end.

The account to lead with

Moroccanoil is the only complete account in the dataset: ads, then site, then two named buyers.

246 paid impressions and 7 clicks in the 180 day window, present in the July engaged cohort at 179 impressions, 2 website visits, and two named people in performance marketing and brand management. 501 to 1,000 employees, squarely inside the ICP band, cosmetics.

Every other account in this report has one or two of those three signals. This is the single clearest proof that the full funnel can work end to end.

01

Method, stated so it can be challenged

Companies
Full industry distribution across all 1,306 identified companies, then exhaustive pulls of the 11 core ICP industries (74 companies, complete set) plus a sweep of the mislabel prone buckets Automotive, Building materials, Wholesale, Consumer services and Design (100 rows).
People, pass A
Seniority filter. CXO returned 95 rows, the complete set. Director, VP, Owner, Partner and Senior returned 100 rows.
People, pass B
Department filter across Marketing, Purchasing and Product management at all seniorities, 57 rows, the complete set. This catches manager level brand and ecommerce people that a seniority filter misses.
Join
Normalised on lowercase, punctuation stripped, corporate suffixes removed (inc, llc, group, usa, brands, the, company), minimum token length 5 to avoid short string false positives.
Label override
WebID industry labels were disregarded where wrong. Moroccanoil, Jane Iredale, Billion Dollar Beauty, Quip, RG Barry and Revlon are all filed under Automotive. Artsana, Beach House Group, Cartograph and Hamilton Beach are under Building materials.
Known limit
The ad exposure list is the top 100 ICP industry companies by impressions, which bottoms out around 235 impressions. Companies below that threshold may have exposure this join cannot see. Hamilton Beach Brands is a confirmed example at 145 July impressions. So 3 matched is a floor and 56 unmatched is a ceiling.

05

What the filter confirms

Overlap

The July ad cohort does appear on the website

Moroccanoil is in both, with two named marketing people. Hamilton Beach Brands is very likely a second case, below the impression cut of the join. The overlap is small but real, which keeps the retargeting premise alive.

Named buyers

Two identified names are confirmed ICP decision makers

Anne Coelen, VP Global Digital Strategy at Beach House Group, and Donna Eberly, Supply Chain Director at Michael Todd Beauty, are both genuine ICP decision makers. A third, Nick Zurek at Skai, is correctly classified as adtech and a non buyer. The named list holds up once the ICP filter is applied properly.

Context

The website population is mostly not the ICP

30 ICP people out of roughly 1,815 identified, and 59 ICP companies out of 1,306, is a low hit rate, and the careers page is the most likely driver. The ICP set is real and workable, but it sits inside a much larger non-ICP population that the account scoring still needs to filter out.

What to do

1 / this week

Upload the 7 Tier 1 accounts as a matched audience

Moroccanoil, Beach House Group, Fekkai, Michael Todd Beauty, Ollie, Cartograph, Artsana USA. Six of the seven have never received a paid impression. Named buyer, right size, right function, demonstrated intent.

2 / this week

Build the wider ICP list into a second audience

The remaining 22 ICP accounts plus the 56 ICP companies without a resolved person. Small, cheap, and currently receiving nothing.

3 / this week

Suppress the 11 competitor entities

Two of them, Podean and Common Thread Collective, sit in the very high engagement tier that carries the ABM proof point in the Pipeline analysis. Removing them will lower the headline numbers, which is why it should be done and explained up front.

4 / before outreach

Enrich the four unknown sizes and verify every URL

Sconza Candy, Real Soda Midwest, Sonoma Roasters and Burly Stone Soap have CEO or owner level visitors and no size data, so they cannot be qualified against the contract floor. Verify all LinkedIn URLs by hand.

5 / next 30 days

Attack the 201 to 500 conversion gap directly

Seven of the 30 named ICP buyers sit in this band, and the audience analysis shows the same band delivering 319 clicks at a healthy CTR with zero leads. The audience is right and engaging on both channels. Fix the offer and the landing experience for this band specifically.

6 / standing rule

Never rank an ICP pull by volume again

Partition by industry, seniority and department. The 100 row cap is hard and silent, and volume ranking systematically hides the buyers, who visit once or twice.
