Why the score is 15, and what unlocks the climb

The hierarchy is not a scorecard of effort. It grades whether each floor is load-bearing, and a floor cannot bear weight until the one beneath it is complete.

The one missing brick / Next 1 / $0

C11 Conversion tracking is the gate to everything above

Four of the five foundation motions are in place, but conversion tracking is not. $18,406 of DemandSense verified spend cannot be tied to revenue, and three sources disagree on conversions. Until it lands, no floor above Foundation can be scored as load-bearing, which is why the account sits in Validate at 15/100.

Already built / Next 2 and 3

The foundation and the first capture moves are ready

SEO and content, founder thought leadership, landing pages and offer architecture are all in place. The moment tracking is live, C8 LinkedIn retargeting and C4 thought-leader amplification become the first two floor-2 moves, both building on assets that already exist.

You cannot skip a floor

Meta, programmatic and ABM stay gated on purpose

Floors 3 and 4 are marked not reachable, not because the motions are wrong but because the signal layer beneath them is incomplete. Scoring or scaling them now would repeat the mistake this account already made on Google — spending into a layer it could not yet measure.
