The number that reframes the whole conversation

Paid media drives 44 of 15,353 website sessions. Organic search drives 4,093. AI assistants drive 389.

Across five months, LinkedIn paid delivered 40 website sessions and Google paid delivered 4. That is 0.29% of all site traffic from 100% of the media budget. Organic search delivered 4,093 sessions, owned email 787, and AI assistants, chiefly ChatGPT, delivered 389 with no spend and no strategy behind it.

Read this correctly. LinkedIn's TOF and MOF campaigns are engagement and document ads with the Audience Network off. Their job is on platform consumption and warm pool building, not clicks to site, so judging them on website sessions is judging them by the wrong metric. That is exactly the point of this diagnostic: each motion has to be measured by its actual job.

What it does prove: the loop from ad, to site, to identification, to pipeline is effectively not running. And the free channels that are working, organic search and AI assistants, have no owner, no budget and no measurement.

03

Channel efficiency scorecard

Each motion judged by its own job, not by a single shared metric.

AUDITTOF, LinkedIn cold

Two campaigns, $12,743, zero leads

60,000 person audience, 102,806 impressions, 3,146 clicks. TL Image runs a 3.87% CTR at $3.65 CPC, TL Video a 2.02% CTR at $5.04. Judged by its job, reach, it is working: this is what fills the retargeting pool. Judged by the audience it reaches, it is the source of the enterprise problem in Report 1. Audit the targeting, not the budget.

AUDITMOF, LinkedIn 90 day retargeting

Three campaigns hitting a 3,500 person pool 28 times each

The Doc, TL Image and lead gen campaigns all target the same 90 day retargeting pool of roughly 3,400 to 3,700 people, and together delivered 97,529 impressions. That is around 28 impressions per pool member across five months. This is the mechanical cause of the CTR decay from 4.44% to 1.30%. The pool is saturated, not the creative exhausted.

MAINTAINMOF, lead gen form

The only motion producing leads

$3,023, 215 clicks, 18 leads at a $167.97 cost per lead. Every lead in the account came from here. It receives 12.3% of LinkedIn spend. It is also sitting in the saturated 90 day pool, so its ceiling is set by pool size rather than by budget.

SCALEMOF, 180 day plus ABM

A 13,500 person pool receiving 1.2 impressions each

Two campaigns, $869 combined, 16,545 impressions against a 13,000 to 14,000 person audience. That is roughly 1.2 impressions per member, against 28 for the 90 day pool. This is the single clearest budget shift available and it costs nothing. Same channel, same account, 23 times less frequency.

SCALEGoogle, now measured

Google outperforms LinkedIn on every metric available

$3,932.69 over 42 days, 18,929 impressions, 2,607 clicks, 13.77% CTR, $1.51 CPC, 2 conversions. Against LinkedIn's 2.79% CTR and $3.80 CPC, Google delivers roughly 5 times the click through rate at 40% of the cost per click, and it is the only channel in the account with recorded conversions. Run rate is $2,809 per month. On this evidence Google should be maintained and scaled, not reduced.

FIXGoogle, the catch

74.5% of Google spend went to affiliate and influencer keywords, and 91% sits in paused ad groups

[amazon affiliate agency] took $1,552.28 for 1,007 clicks and 1 conversion. [amazon influencer affiliate program] took $1,332.61 for 901 clicks and zero. Together with the creator connections term that is $2,929.82 of $3,932.69. The Amazon Affiliate/Creator and Amazon DSP ad groups, now both paused, hold $3,578 of the spend, and three of seven ad groups have never spent a dollar. A 12.51% CTR on non brand B2B search is not a win, it is an intent mismatch: those terms are searched by people wanting to become an Amazon affiliate, not by brands hiring an agency. This is the keyword report, not the search terms report, so confirm before acting.

SCALEOrganic, no spend

Organic search is the largest real acquisition channel

4,093 sessions and 583 identified companies, 27% of site traffic, against 44 sessions from all paid combined. Content dwell confirms depth: the Ollie case study holds 383 seconds, /case-studies/ 118, /amazon-growth-services/ 99. This is compounding and unowned.

NEXTAI assistants, no spend

389 sessions from ChatGPT, Copilot and Perplexity

Ten times what the entire paid programme delivers to the website, at zero cost. The April white space analysis named AI driven commerce discovery as competitive white space. The traffic now proves the thesis. This turns the deferred ChatGPT ads proposal from an upsell into a test of demand already arriving.

OPTIMIZEOwned email

787 sessions and not connected to paid at all

Envision Horizons branded email drives 687 sessions, affiliates 42, partner newsletters and AMAC sponsorships the rest. A 20,000 plus subscriber newsletter exists and has never been uploaded as a matched audience. The largest warm pool in the business is invisible to the ad account.

Warm saturation verdict

No. Warm is not saturated, it is badly distributed.

One pool of 3,500 people is being hit 28 times each while a pool of 13,500 gets 1.2 times and three further pools, including a 20,000 subscriber newsletter, have never been activated at all. The account does not need more budget to fix this and it should not add a cold channel until it is fixed. Rebalancing existing spend across pools that already exist is the highest return action available and it costs nothing.

05

The roadmap

Now, next, later. No phase is skipped, and the data decides when each activates.

Phase 1 / now

Optimise

Zero incremental cost. Reallocation only.

  1. Shift MOF budget from the 90 day pool to the 180 day plus ABM pool. 28x against 1.2x on the same channel.
  2. Upload the newsletter list, website identified accounts and the ABM list as matched audiences. Three untapped pools, one afternoon of work.
  3. Build a real BOF motion. Six of seven BOF rows are gaps. Start with an audit or teardown offer behind the lead gen form.
  4. Fix conversion tracking via the Zapier to LinkedIn offline bridge, plus a thank you page.
  5. Exclude careers paths from identification. 6,887 views and 489 companies of noise removed in one change.
  6. Apply audience exclusions. Exclude internal, non-buyer and competitor accounts from targeting and audience scoring.

Phase 2 / next

Expand

Meta at $2,500 per month. Activates once Phase 1 is done.

  1. Meta amplification against the enriched ABM list. Frequency extension for accounts LinkedIn already reached, at a fraction of LinkedIn CPMs.
  2. Meta for the Prime Day and Shopify ICP. The lower ACV segment that is less active on LinkedIn.
  3. Category specific tactical playbooks as the creative payload, per Report 4. Best format crossed with best category.
  4. Readiness signal: do not start until conversion tracking fires and the CRM join exists, or Meta will be as unmeasurable as Google is now.

Phase 3 / later

Scale

AI assistant ads at $750 per month, plus programmatic.

  1. AI assistant ads. 389 sessions already arriving unpaid. Test whether it scales.
  2. Programmatic frequency extension for the Tier 1 named accounts, measured by presence rather than last touch.
  3. Formalise the affiliate and partner motion. The affiliate page holds 174 seconds of dwell and already drives 42 sessions with no programme behind it.
  4. Content investment against organic search, the largest real acquisition channel in the business and currently unowned.
  5. Readiness signal: Phase 1 and 2 optimised, and pipeline attribution demonstrably working.

Bottom line

Phase 1 moves $1,763 inside the existing budget and adds a funnel stage that does not currently exist.

No increase, no new channel, no new contract. It takes money out of a pool being hit 28 times and puts it into a pool being hit 1.2 times, kills two motions producing nothing, and funds the missing bottom of the funnel.

And the actions that matter most are not budget actions at all. Uploading the newsletter list, uploading the website identified accounts, fixing conversion tracking, excluding careers traffic and applying the audience exclusions cost nothing and would change every number in the next report. Any conversation about adding channels should come second.
