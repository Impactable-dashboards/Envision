Path forward / 90 days

90 Day Roadmap What happens now, next and later. Owners, triggers, and the budget that moves without a new dollar.

From 29 July 2026  /  sequenced across three phases  /  prepared by Impactable

$0 Phase 1 cost $1,763 reallocated 6 free actions first 3 warm pools to activate 2 expansion channels
The argument

The first thirty days cost nothing and change every number in the next report.

Uploading three warm audiences that already exist, rebalancing spend between two pools on the same channel, excluding careers traffic from identification, suppressing the client's own company and eleven competitors, and getting conversion tracking to fire. None of it requires a budget increase or a new channel.

Expansion comes second, and it is gated on measurement working. Adding Meta or AI ads to an account that cannot verify a single conversion would produce a second unmeasurable channel rather than growth.

01

Phase 1: days 1 to 30, optimise

Zero incremental cost. Every action is a reallocation, an upload, or a fix.

Week 1

Rebalance the retargeting pools

Owner: Andrii Snizhko  /  Trigger: none, do it now

Move budget from the 90 day pool, currently at roughly 28 impressions per member, to the 180 day plus ABM pool at 1.2. Doc RT to $400, TL Image RT to $400, 180D plus ABM from $177 to $1,180. Same channel, same account, 23 times less frequency on the receiving side.

Week 1

Apply the suppression list

Owner: Andrii Snizhko  /  Trigger: none

Both Envision Horizons entities including the marketingconcepts.com duplicate, Impactable, Amazon, and eleven competitor and agency entities including Podean, Common Thread Collective, Bebold Digital and Market Defense. Two sit in the very high engagement tier, so the headline account numbers will drop. Say so before the next report shows it.

Week 1

Exclude careers traffic from identification

Owner: Impactable, client site access needed  /  Trigger: none

Three paths: /careers/, /career-page-new/, and the 2022 theresumator_helper.html file that is the second most viewed page on the entire site. Together 6,887 views and 489 identified companies. Single highest leverage hygiene fix in the account.

Week 1

Pause the affiliate and influencer keywords

Owner: Darko Manic and Milovan Kolakovic  /  Trigger: none

$2,929.82 of $3,932.69 Google spend went to [amazon affiliate agency], [amazon influencer affiliate program] and the creator connections term. Those are searched by people wanting to become an affiliate. Pull the search terms report first to confirm, then pause or add negatives. This is the single largest identified waste in the account.

Week 1

Require a business email on the lead form

Owner: Todd Piechowski with Impactable  /  Trigger: client action

Ten of seventeen LinkedIn leads have no company and a personal address. Blocking free email providers on the gated download converts the lead count from a vanity number into a qualified one. Expect volume to fall and quality to rise, and say so before it happens.

Week 2

Upload the three untapped warm pools

Owner: Andrii with Tristan Williams  /  Trigger: client list delivery

The 20,000 plus newsletter list, the 1,325 website identified companies, and the full 794 company ABM list, of which 549 have now been reached at 69.1% penetration, leaving 246 never reached. The largest warm pool in the business has never been visible to the ad account.

Week 2

Fix conversion tracking

Owner: Todd Piechowski, supported by Milovan  /  Trigger: client action

Zapier to LinkedIn offline conversion bridge, a dedicated thank you page to fire the signal, and shorten the two minute form. Graduation gate: 10 or more conversion events per month. Until this fires, no ROAS conversation is possible.

Week 3

Swap the creative that is manufacturing the audience problem

Owner: Impactable creative  /  Trigger: none

Retire the Estee Lauder, Harry's and Borghese case studies from paid distribution. Replace with the Ollie case study, which already holds 383 seconds of average dwell on the site and features a mid market DTC brand. Cut Founder Video, PostPrimeDay and the webinar image: $1,950 at zero leads.

Week 3

Build the first real bottom of funnel motion

Owner: Steven Doub with Impactable  /  Trigger: client asset or our value add

Six of seven BOF rows are gaps. Fund $477 per month from the reallocation. Start with an audit or teardown offer behind the lead gen form rather than another gated document. This is the missing stage, not an optimisation.

Week 4

Ship the Tier 1 named account outreach

Owner: Steven Doub to Laura's sales motion  /  Trigger: hand verification

Seven accounts with a named decision maker: Moroccanoil (two marketing managers, the only complete three signal account), Beach House Group, Fekkai, Michael Todd Beauty, Ollie, Cartograph, Artsana USA. Verify every LinkedIn URL by hand first, two records elsewhere link to the wrong person.

Week 4

Scale the one angle that converts

Owner: Impactable creative  /  Trigger: none

Three new variants of the seasonal tactical playbook, $74.15 CPL and every recorded conversion on $371 of spend. Use variant 1_B as the template, not 1_A. Q4 and holiday are the next windows. Build one category specific version for wellness and supplements.
