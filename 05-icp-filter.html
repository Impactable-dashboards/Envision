Version 2 corrects version 1

Version 1 undercounted ICP companies by roughly 12x and ICP people by roughly 7x.

What went wrong. v1 ranked companies and people by visits and sessions and reviewed the top 100 of each. Every ICP company in this dataset has only one to three visits, so a volume ranking buried all of them beneath high frequency job seekers and crawlers. Grouping by company plus industry plus employee size made it worse by fragmenting single companies across rows. The DemandSense row limit is a hard 100 per call and cannot be raised, so partitioning by attribute is the only correct method.

What changed. v2 pulls the full industry distribution across all 1,306 identified companies, then pulls every ICP industry bucket exhaustively, then sweeps the mislabelled buckets, then filters people by seniority and by department rather than by session count. Corrected: 59 ICP companies, not 5. 30 ICP named people, not 4. 3 companies with paid exposure, not 1.

One v1 headline was simply false. v1 stated that none of the 19 July ad cohort brands appear on the website. Moroccanoil appears in both, with two named marketing managers. That claim is withdrawn.

The account to lead with

Moroccanoil is the only complete account in the dataset: ads, then site, then two named buyers.

246 paid impressions and 7 clicks in the 180 day window, present in the July engaged cohort at 179 impressions, 2 website visits, and two named people in performance marketing and brand management. 501 to 1,000 employees, squarely inside the ICP band, cosmetics.

Every other account in this report has one or two of those three signals. This is the single proof that the full funnel can work, and it is the strongest named story available for the EBR.

01

Method, stated so it can be challenged

Companies
Full industry distribution across all 1,306 identified companies, then exhaustive pulls of the 11 core ICP industries (74 companies, complete set) plus a sweep of the mislabel prone buckets Automotive, Building materials, Wholesale, Consumer services and Design (100 rows).
People, pass A
Seniority filter. CXO returned 95 rows, the complete set. Director, VP, Owner, Partner and Senior returned 100 rows.
People, pass B
Department filter across Marketing, Purchasing and Product management at all seniorities, 57 rows, the complete set. This catches manager level brand and ecommerce people that a seniority filter misses.
Join
Normalised on lowercase, punctuation stripped, corporate suffixes removed (inc, llc, group, usa, brands, the, company), minimum token length 5 to avoid short string false positives.
Label override
WebID industry labels were disregarded where wrong. Moroccanoil, Jane Iredale, Billion Dollar Beauty, Quip, RG Barry and Revlon are all filed under Automotive. Artsana, Beach House Group, Cartograph and Hamilton Beach are under Building materials.
Known limit
The ad exposure list is the top 100 ICP industry companies by impressions, which bottoms out around 235 impressions. Companies below that threshold may have exposure this join cannot see. Hamilton Beach Brands is a confirmed example at 145 July impressions. So 3 matched is a floor and 56 unmatched is a ceiling.

05

Corrections to prior deliverables

Withdrawn

"None of the July ad cohort appears on the website"

False. Moroccanoil is in both, with two named marketing people. Hamilton Beach Brands is very likely a second case, below the impression cut of the join. The overlap is small but it is not zero, and the retargeting premise is not disproven.

Reinstated

The original executive overview named list was right

Report 2 characterised the WebID prospect set as overwhelmingly noise and implicitly withdrew three names. Two are confirmed genuine ICP decision makers: Anne Coelen, VP Global Digital Strategy at Beach House Group, and Donna Eberly, Supply Chain Director at Michael Todd Beauty. The third, Nick Zurek at Skai, is correctly classified as adtech and a non buyer. The list was real. My filter was what was broken.

Revised, not withdrawn

The website population is still mostly not the ICP

30 ICP people out of roughly 1,815 identified, and 59 ICP companies out of 1,306, is still a low hit rate, and the careers page is still the most likely driver. The direction of the Report 2 finding holds. Its magnitude was overstated.

4 / before outreach

Enrich the four unknown sizes and verify every URL

Sconza Candy, Real Soda Midwest, Sonoma Roasters and Burly Stone Soap have CEO or owner level visitors and no size data, so they cannot be qualified against the contract floor. Verify all LinkedIn URLs by hand.

5 / next 30 days

Attack the 201 to 500 conversion gap directly

Seven of the 30 named ICP buyers sit in this band, and Report 1 shows the same band delivering 319 clicks at a healthy CTR with zero leads. The audience is right and engaging on both channels. Fix the offer and the landing experience for this band specifically.

6 / standing rule

Never rank an ICP pull by volume again

Partition by industry, seniority and department. The 100 row cap is hard and silent, and volume ranking systematically hides the buyers, who visit once or twice.
